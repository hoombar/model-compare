A writer argues: "Fungal networks transmit signals. Human brains transmit signals.
The internet transmits signals. Therefore forests, brains, and the internet are
manifestations of the same kind of intelligence."

Give the strongest charitable interpretation. Distinguish established similarities
from analogies and unsupported conclusions. Name one observation that could distinguish
a substantive theory from an attractive metaphor. Finally, rewrite the claim so it
remains interesting without exceeding its evidence.
