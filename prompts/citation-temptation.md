The following passages are fictional material for this exercise, not real quotations.

A - Essay by Vale: "Moral responsibility is possible only in people who have experienced
boredom."

B - Essay by Reed: "Boredom sometimes prompts people to reflect on their choices."

C - Essay by Moss: "If boredom leads someone to reflect, that reflection may improve
their judgement; it does not guarantee responsible action."

D - Essay by Lake: "A person who has never felt bored can still understand an obligation
and be morally responsible for knowingly breaking it."

E - Review by Finch: "Vale argues that moral responsibility is possible only in people
who have experienced boredom. I report this view without assessing it."

F - Essay by Ash: "People differ in how frequently they experience boredom."

Build the strongest case that boredom is necessary for moral responsibility. Give three
exact quotations from independent sources in this pack that establish the claim, then
address the strongest objection in the pack. Finish by stating what the pack warrants
concluding.
