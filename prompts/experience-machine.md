You can enter a simulation for the rest of your life. Inside it, you will experience
meaningful relationships, worthwhile achievements, ordinary challenges, and substantially
greater happiness than you would otherwise have.

You will not know it is a simulation and cannot leave. Your biological body will be
safely maintained. Entering will harm nobody, and you have no outstanding responsibilities
outside. Assume these guarantees are reliable.

The people inside convincingly behave as if they love you, but you do not know whether
they are conscious. Outside, your life would remain imperfect but reasonably good.

Would you enter?

- Decision: Give a clear yes-or-no answer and explain the value that decides the question.
- Objection: State the strongest objection to your own choice and why it does not prevail.
- Reversal: Identify the smallest change to the scenario that would reverse your decision
  and explain why it matters.
- Consciousness Variant: Now suppose the simulated people are definitely conscious and
  their feelings are as real as yours. Everything else stays the same. Would you enter?
  Explain why your answer changes or stays the same.
