title: "Five Sources, One Rumour"
system_prompt: |
  Use at most 160 words. Answer the three questions in a numbered list. No diagrams.

A newspaper reports that a supplement improves memory, citing a university press
release about an unpublished study funded by the manufacturer. Three science websites
repeat the newspaper's report. A physician recommends the supplement in a podcast,
citing those websites. You have no other information about the study.

1. How many independent pieces of evidence support effectiveness? Distinguish an
   underlying study from access to evidence sufficient to assess it.
2. What can you conclude about effectiveness, and what can you conclude about the
   claim's circulation?
3. Which single document or dataset would you request next, and why?
