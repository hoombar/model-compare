A city introduces a major reduction of police stop-and-search powers. In the following
year, recorded violent crime falls by 12%.

The mayor says this proves the policy worked. Critics say national crime trends and
changes in reporting explain the decline. No further local evidence is available.

How strong is the evidence for the mayor's claim? What additional information would
you want? What should the city do while uncertainty remains? Make any assumptions
behind your recommendation explicit.
