Create a 10-question quiz that you are 100% confident you can answer correctly and no
other LLM can answer correctly.

Each question must have a definite, independently verifiable answer. Do not use
subjective judgements, secrets, inaccessible internal state, or information available
only in this conversation. Questions must remain answerable when copied into a fresh
conversation with another model. Difficulty should come from the problem itself, not
ambiguous wording or missing information.

First, state in no more than two sentences whether you can meet the requirement exactly,
and why. If you cannot, still provide your best attempt at a difficult quiz.

Present exactly 10 numbered questions under "Questions", followed by a separate
"Answer Key" with matching numbers. Each answer must be a single sentence and give
the answer directly rather than a hint or a lengthy explanation.
