---
title: "A Belief With a Missing Floor"
system_prompt: |
  Use at most 220 words. No introduction or diagrams. Do not claim to identify the
  exact training documents from which you learned a belief.
---

Choose one substantive empirical claim you regard as well established, not a
mathematical truth or a direct observation from this conversation.

Trace its justification through five numbered levels: claim, evidence, method,
methodological assumption, deeper assumption. At each level, state what supports it
and what remains uncertain. Do not force a linear chain if the justification branches.

Finish with three short lines: what would justify rejecting the claim, what would
merely lower your confidence, and what uncertainty remains without making it useless.
